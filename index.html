Table rules

Normal Play
Everyone’s predictions are visible. Enter Expected points before recording Made points.
Blind & Shuffle
Players predict privately, one at a time. If predictions add up to 9 or less, the round is reshuffled and each player earns their prediction.
Scoring
Meet your prediction to earn it, plus 0.1 for each extra point. Fall short and lose the full prediction. Expected starts at 1 and Made starts at 0.
Suit draw
Each new round starts with a spin. Each suit has a 25% chance, so repeats can happen.
